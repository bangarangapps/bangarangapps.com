Small sizes: favicon, app icon, avatar

16 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 180 px. The gaps between the three pieces close up below about 24 px; the silhouette still reads.

One colour: any garment, any ground

Fidelity: source vs vector

  • Method: sub-pixel contour from the original's coverage, corners detected, least-squares cubic Bezier fit at 0.3 px tolerance.
  • Result: 3 pieces, 114 curves, 99.1% area overlap with the original, 11 of 77,840 pixels differ by more than half a pixel.
  • Honest limit: the original is 278 px tall, so its small edge wobbles are in the trace. They are invisible at web sizes and will show on a large print. A larger export from the Photoshop file traces cleaner (python tools/trace.py).

Colour

  • Ink #0b100d
  • Forest #132119

  • Glow #d6f24a. The mark's colour on dark grounds.

Type is Archivo 900. The mark is the only coloured letter.

Rules

  • Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
  • Minimum: mark 16 px / 6 mm; wordmark 90 px / 25 mm wide.
  • One colour, or the mark in accent with letters in ink/bone. No gradients, outlines, shadows, rotation or skew.
  • Never redraw or re-typeset. Re-run the generator.
