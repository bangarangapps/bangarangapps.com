Stacked lockup (plain A beside the mark, so it isn't repeated)

Two optical cuts: display and small

  • Display is the artwork as drawn. Use it above about 40 px: hero, print, merch.
  • Small doubles the gaps between the three pieces so they survive at 16 to 36 px, with looser letter-spacing and more air to the right of the mark so the R no longer crowds it. Used for the header, footer and favicon.
  • Same drawing, same flow and right-hand line; only the gap width and spacing change.

Small sizes: favicon, app icon, avatar

16 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 180 px, small cut. At 32 px all three pieces separate; at 16 px it reads as a sail silhouette with stripes.

One colour: any garment, any ground

Construction: source vs vector

  • Right edge: cap and band edges sit on one straight line (51.5 degrees, apex to the swoosh's top-right corner). The band's top-right is a sharp corner on that line; the swoosh curve starts there.
  • Gaps: identical, 11 px at source scale, constant along their length (measured 10.8 to 11.1 px). Each gap is an exact offset of the edge above it, so the pieces flow.
  • Points: 11 Bezier segments for the whole mark, smooth except at true corners. A raw pixel trace needed 114 and was bumpy.
  • Fidelity: mean 0.85 px from your original, max 3.1 px of 248 (1.3%). The 278 px original has wobble; this keeps its design, not its wobble.

Colour

  • Ink #0c0a0a
  • Oxblood #210d10
  • Bone #eee9da
  • Red #ff1f2d. Highlighter red: the mark's colour on dark grounds, and the full-bleed finale.

Wordmark type is Gabarito Bold (SIL OFL), cap height normalised, tracking 0. The mark is the only coloured letter.

Rules

  • Clear space: a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
  • Minimum: mark 16 px / 6 mm; wordmark 90 px / 25 mm wide.
  • One colour, or the mark in accent with letters in ink/bone. No gradients, outlines, shadows, rotation or skew.
  • Never redraw or re-typeset. Re-run the generator.
