# Bangarang Apps — brand

**Who:** Bangarang Apps, Inc. An app studio building sharp, modern apps in categories that got comfortable. Current products: SimpleInvite, fdbck.

**Voice:** light, quick, a little mischievous, never smug. Hints that we want to do it better and put some life back into a market, never a word against anyone. Short sentences. No filler sublines. Confident, not cheeky.
- The name is the nod. Never reference the film, never use pirate/nautical language ("ship" as in ship apps is fine).
- **Never state a headcount** (no "two of us", no "two founders") and **never frame the product line as small or finished** (no "two so far", "just two"). Say "Out now", not "two so far".
- **Never mention agents, AI, vibe coding or similar anywhere on the site**, including product copy (fdbck's panel is written without it). Hint at modern tooling only: "modern tools", "the newest tools we can get our hands on".
- **No exclamation mark in the brand.** The wordmark is plain BANGARANG.
- **Say "apps", never "software".**
- Never name or criticise an incumbent. "Some app categories got comfortable" is as pointed as it gets.

**Mark:** the designer's own artwork (a three-panel sail), rebuilt as clean vectors by `tools/construct.py` from `tools/source/mark-original.png` under three rules the owner set: (1) the right-hand edges of the cap and the band lie on ONE straight line (apex to the swoosh's top-right corner), the band's top-right is a sharp corner on that line, and the swoosh's curve starts at that corner; (2) the two gaps are exactly the same width (11 px at source scale) and parallel along their whole length, so the three pieces read as one continuous flow; (3) proportions and curvature come from the original. 11 Bezier segments in total, smooth (tangent-continuous) except at true corners. Not a raw trace: a pixel trace of the 278 px original kept its wobble and was rejected as bumpy. It replaces the middle A of the name. **Never redraw, stretch, skew, split or recolour pieces of it; uniform scale only.** **Two optical cuts:** display (gaps as drawn, 11 px at source scale) for hero/large/print; small (22 px gaps, tracking +3%, extra air right of the mark) for header, footer and favicon, so the three pieces survive at 16-36 px and the R doesn't crowd the mark. Same geometry otherwise. Build the small mark with `python tools/construct.py --gap 22 --out mark-path-small.json -q`. Generated formats in `assets/brand/`: `mark.svg`, `wordmark.svg`, `wordmark-plain.svg`, `lockup-stacked.svg`; plus `/favicon.svg`, `/apple-touch-icon.png`, `/og.png`. Regenerate: `tools/construct.py` then `tools/wordmark.py`, `tools/inject.py`, `tools/sheet.py` (rebuilds `docs/brand-sheet.html`, the one-page brand sheet). To change the design, change the constants/anchors at the top of `construct.py` (GAP, corner anchors), not the SVGs.

**Visual:** one stylesheet, color in "worlds". Studio worlds: ink `#0c0a0a`, oxblood `#210d10`, bone `#eee9da`, **highlighter red `#ff1f2d`** (the mark, the accent, the finale; a nod to Rufio's hair, never named). Buttons on red use ink text (white fails contrast). Product panels wear the product's own colors, and the header sail and CTA follow them (see "Per-product header accent" below). Product sections wear the product's own identity: SimpleInvite = paper `#efeae0` + ink `#211d16`, card mark always black, CTA blue `#0f3fa8`, Young Serif/Hanken; fdbck = night `#0a0a0b` + amber `#d97706` + Outfit/DM Sans. Wordmark: **Gabarito Bold** (SIL OFL; vendored in `tools/source/fonts`; chosen from `docs/wordmark-options.html`). Site type: Archivo for headings/body, JetBrains Mono for labels.
- Motion is scroll-linked. **The logo is never animated: no squish, stretch, morph or colour sweep.** Hero interaction is depth only: the eyebrow and statement drift up slightly faster than the pinned logo.
- Motion rules otherwise: horizontal/vertical only. No tilt, no skew, no stacked or overlapping objects, no fake materials (house rule). Reduced motion = static page.
- Real imagery only: product shots are 2x captures of the live products (`assets/img`). Re-capture when a product's UI changes.
- Never Fraunces. No gradients.

**Gotchas learned:**
- `.wrap` is margin-based. Never put a `padding` shorthand on an element that also carries `.wrap` (v1 bug: it zeroed the mobile gutter).
- Cloudflare Browser Integrity Check was blocking Stripe's site-verification crawler; it is OFF on this zone. Leave it off.

## Per-product header accent
While a product panel is under the header, the sail and "Say hello" button take that product's own accent: SimpleInvite `#0f3fa8` (its CTA blue), fdbck `#d97706` (amber). Everywhere else: brand red. The SimpleInvite panel mark is the product's card mark (their repo: `packages/design/src/brand/card.ts`, final as of origin/main 441a319d, confirmed by their logo session 2026-10-16): CARD_FACE filled, CARD_PATH stroked 24 with round caps/joins, `overflow: visible`. Black `#111`, never an accent. Set at THEIR lockup proportions: card 1.177em tall, .306em gap, seated .04em low, word in Young Serif. App icon: the card in white on a full-bleed cobalt `#0F3FA8` tile (their `icon.svg`; not `favicon.svg`, a separate tab-icon file). `tools/si_mark.py` pulls all of it from their origin/main, writes `assets/brand/simpleinvite-card.svg` and `simpleinvite-icon.svg`, and patches the homepage. Old envelope mark: superseded, do not use.

## Policies and payments (Stripe site verification)
Stripe's business-website check wants: business name, what is sold, customer-service contact, refund/dispute/cancellation policy, no password, no region blocks, not under construction. This site satisfies it with `/refunds/` (cancellation, refunds, disputes, tax, restrictions for both products), a "Products and payments" section in `/terms/`, and three contact addresses. Keep `/refunds/` in step with SimpleInvite's and fdbck's own terms (SimpleInvite: $5/event, $39/year, no auto-renew; fdbck: Pro subscription, cancel anytime, keep until period end). Anchor links (`#apps`, `#hello`...) land flush at the top (`scroll-margin-top`) so the header takes the target's color world.

## Lockup proportions (shipped 2026-10-14)
Module u = 50 units: cap 14u (700), stem 3u (150), mark 18u (900) tall = 6 stems, overhang 2u above and below the caps. Display-cut pads: 0.0297 / 0.0907 of cap, set so the optical (area) gap on each side of the mark is 10% over the typeface's own A (242 / 200 vs 220 / 182), keeping the typeface's own left:right asymmetry (1.21). Small cut (header, footer, favicon) keeps its wider pads. Measure with `tools/proportions.py`; full review with annotated blocks in `docs/lockup-proportions.html` (rebuilt by `tools/proportions_sheet.py`); the pre-change files are in `docs/lockup-before/`.

## Hero sail
One oversized sail (the display mark, `svg.hero-sail`, kept in sync by `tools/inject.py`) sits behind the hero type at 11% opacity in `--accent`, 94svh tall, bleeding 30% off the right edge. Static, `aria-hidden`, behind the text (`z-index:0`). The hero stage is `overflow: clip` so it never creates sideways scroll. Do not animate it and do not repeat it elsewhere.

## The rotating word
The one word in our line rotates. "Apps with a little more **life** in them." (hero), "...deserve a little **life**:" (manifesto) and "More **life**." (strip) all share one current word. It starts on a random word on every load (life is one of the pool). Words come from a shuffled deck remembered in localStorage (`bg-rw-deck`), so no word repeats until all 16 have been shown, across reloads and in-page rotation alike. The pool: spark, zest, zing, snap, kick, buzz, pulse, charm, flair, verve, nerve, soul, play, joy, wit. Hero: rolls live, in a red highlighter box (selection style, ink text), every 3.4 s while on screen. Manifesto and strip: swap only while off screen, so text never reflows in view. Reduced motion: a random word per load, no rotation. No JS: "life". Static metadata (title, meta, OG) stays "life".
Rules for new words: a mass noun that fits both "a little more ___ in them" and "a little ___:" and "More ___."; five letters or fewer (phone hero line must stay on one line at 320 px; "charm" is the widest); light, quick, a little mischievous, never smug; no pirate/nautical words; no tech/AI words. Edit the list in `assets/js/site.js` (`RW_WORDS`).
