01 · The mark

A sail that
is also an A

Three panels in one continuous flow, standing in for the middle A of the name. It is the designer's own artwork rebuilt as clean vectors, not a trace. Uniform scale only: never redraw, stretch, skew, split, recolor pieces, or animate it.

Two optical cuts, one geometry

  • Display cut: gaps as drawn. Hero, print, large uses, 48 px and up.
  • Small cut: wider gaps (2x), looser tracking, more air after the mark so the R never crowds it. Header, footer, favicon, anything under about 40 px.
  • The mark stands 18 units tall on a 50-unit module (cap 14, stem 3, overhang 2 each side) and hangs equally above and below the letters. Spacing was set by area: both gaps sit 10% over the typeface's own A.

Construction rules (set by the owner)

  • The right-hand edges of the cap and the band sit on one straight line; the swoosh's curve starts at the band's sharp top-right corner.
  • The two gaps are exactly the same width and parallel for their whole length.
  • Proportions and curvature come from the original artwork. 11 Bezier segments, smooth except at true corners.
02 · Wordmark and lockups

The name with
a sail in it

Gabarito Bold outlines, cap height normalized, the mark replacing the middle A. Always set from the supplied files; never retype it in a font.

Clear space and minimums

  • Keep clear space of one mark-width on every side.
  • Wordmark: never under 90 px wide on screen. The mark alone: never under 16 px, and use the small cut below 40 px.
  • No exclamation mark, ever. The name is plain BANGARANG.

The header sail follows the product

  • Brand red everywhere by default.
  • #0f3fa8 while a SimpleInvite section is under the header.
  • #d97706 while an fdbck section is under the header.
  • The sail changes color between sections as you scroll. The logo itself never animates.

07 · Motion and layout

It moves as
you scroll

Motion

  • DoScroll-linked: words light up in the manifesto, strips drift sideways, product shots parallax, sections reveal.
  • DoHorizontal and vertical movement only. Reduced motion means a static, fully readable page.
  • NeverAnimate the logo: no squish, stretch, morph or color sweep.

Layout and imagery

  • NeverTilt, skew, stack or overlap elements. No fake materials: no tape, torn edges, perforations.
  • NeverGradients, Fraunces, stock imagery or placeholder shots.
  • DoThe hero carries one oversized sail at 11% opacity behind the type, bleeding off the right edge. It is static and decorative; never add a second one.
  • DoReal captures of the live products at 2x; re-capture when their UI changes.
  • DoAnchor links land flush so the header takes the section's color immediately.
